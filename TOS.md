## What the analyzer provides

The analyzer helps you query RVTools exports and prepare infrastructure health checks, migration assessments, cloud sizing, licensing estimates, and bills of materials. It is a planning aid based on a point-in-time export, the assumptions selected for the analysis, and the available vendor information.

Reports are not vendor certification, a support commitment, a binding quote, or an authoritative licensing determination. An export may be incomplete or outdated, and AI-generated explanations can contain errors. A migration marked eligible is not a guarantee that it will succeed.

Before acting on a report, validate the inputs, workload measurements, scope, sizing assumptions, resilience and storage policies, current vendor support requirements, and licensing agreements. Confirm prices, taxes, discounts, regional availability, and contractual terms with the relevant provider. Keep backups and use your organization's change-approval process before changing production systems.
